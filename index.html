Regulatory Context & Vital Significance

Eliminating the legal grey area, standardizing Vietnam's digital asset market

Decree 284/2026/ND-CP is a milestone legal document, marking Vietnam's first comprehensive and strict legal framework for Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs). The decree completely ends the era of voluntary self-regulation, bringing asset transfers, custody, and digital asset trading closer to international standards of transparency, capital flow management, and end-user protection.

Frequently Asked Legal & Regulatory Questions

Decree 284 Regulatory Inquiries

01 Are individual traders using international foreign exchanges subject to direct penalties?
Decree 284/2026/ND-CP primarily regulates corporate and legal entities operating as Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs). Individual investors are not barred from holding digital assets; however, fiat conversion withdrawals via domestic banking channels require proper personal tax reporting and verifiable capital provenance under amended AML guidelines.
02 What are the core prerequisite conditions for technology companies to obtain a VASP license?
Applicants must fulfill four criteria: statutory paid-in charter capital minimums per tier; local server data storage located within Vietnam for at least 5 years; integration of standardized eKYC and Travel Rule solutions; executive compliance accreditation with clean financial background verification.
03 Do virtual attendees receive the blockchain-verified CPE E-Certificate?
Yes. Virtual participants maintaining at least 80% active attendance throughout conference sessions will receive an official Certificate of Completion (E-Certificate) encoded with a tamper-proof blockchain verification hash.
